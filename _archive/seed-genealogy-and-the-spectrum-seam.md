## Worked example — the tetragrammatron tetrahedron

Method (provenance): the author models the shape while reciting the words —
reading scripture as pseudocode, reciting-and-shaping to **infer the function**
behind the literal. That is hypothesis-generation: it proposes a candidate
operation; the operation becomes knowledge only when it can say no. Here the
inference pointed at something real, and the check held.

The four literal vertices spell WORD / GOD / OF / of (WORD OF GOD; GOD IS WORD).
Built from the raw bytes, by the author's own tetra-mask rule (equal length only
is comparable):

- **One literal edge:** `OF ↔ of = 0x20 0x20`, popcount 2 — the case bit on each
  letter: lower↔upper, the promote/deprecate tag functor (the Shift bit). The one
  relation present in the unpadded literals is a single bit.
- **Five edges are not comparable** — WORD(4), GOD(3), OF/of(2) differ in length —
  so they need the display/padding layer. That layer *is* the author's pipeline:
  `cat -An` (pads with line numbers), `diff -y` (aligns side by side), `rev`
  (flips). The pipeline is not decoration; it is the padding that makes the
  unequal literals into a comparable tetrahedron.
- **The centroid is the predicate** — IS / OF — the relation that associates the
  vertices (S-P-O: the predicate at the centre). It is *produced by* the display
  layer, not inherent in the raw bytes; only the OF/of case-bit edge is literal
  unpadded. So the associable centroid is an artifact of the padding, and that is
  exact — it can say no.

Seam: "a literal box with a hole, as a comma" holds at the glyph level (the box is
the container, the hole the one opening, the comma the smallest delimiter that
both separates and joins); it is a reading until pinned to bytes. The byte-level
literals that check are the `0x20` case edge and the padding role of the pipeline.
"It spells GOD" is overlay laid on perceivable geometry.
