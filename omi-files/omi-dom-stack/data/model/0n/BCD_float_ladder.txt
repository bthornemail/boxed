BCD FLOAT LADDER — 0n literal form
Every row carries the 0n suffix: a sign bit, an exponent, and a significand.
The significand is the modern term; the mantissa is what remains after the
implicit leading bit is stripped. 0x0011n decomposes as:

  sign       = 1 bit          (top of the nibble)
  exponent   = byte.offset    (_1d/0-atomics/1-byte.offset)
  significand= bytes.per.element (_1d/0-atomics/0-bytes.per.element)
  span       = byte.length    (_1d/0-atomics/2-byte.length)

Repunit family (octal) — jump vectors, 0n form:

0b0001(/CONSTRAINT/)0o0001n
0b0011((/CONSTRAINT/g))0o0002n
0b0111(((/CONSTRAINT/BOUNDRY/)))0o0003n
0b1111((((/CONSTRAINT/BOUNDRY/g))))0o0004n
